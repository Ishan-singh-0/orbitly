What's inside

  • Artist portfolio pagesImage uploads, campus and country, products and reviews.
  • A real customiserColour, size, quantity and artwork, with a preview before the request is sent.
  • Orders and honest reviewsRequested, in progress, delivered, completed. Only buyers of completed orders can review.
  • Messaging in real timeConversations and live updates over server-sent events.
  • Stripe checkoutHosted checkout with signed webhooks, switched on with keys. Payouts and refunds are admin actions.
  • SecurityStrict CSP, scrypt, CSRF protection, optional two-step login, a self-hosted proof-of-work check.
  • Admin consoleMembers, listings, orders, payments and reports.
  • No dependenciesPlain Node and plain JavaScript. Nothing to install.
