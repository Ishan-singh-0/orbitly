How it works

You talk to the artist directly and agree everything together. Nothing is hidden behind a form.

  1. 01

    Pick a product

    Browse bags, desk gear, clothing, mugs and prints, in the colours and sizes that suit you.

  2. 02

    Choose an artist

    Look through portfolios from campuses and countries around the world. Open any page to see the work first.

  3. 03

    Customise it

    Choose a colour, a size and a piece of the artist's work, and see a preview before you send the request.

  4. 04

    Agree and receive

    Settle the details in chat, pay if online payment is on, approve the delivery and leave a review.
