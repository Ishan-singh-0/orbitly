How it works

You talk to the artist directly and agree everything together. Nothing is hidden behind a form.

  1. 01

    Tell us what you have

    A bag, a mouse, a hoodie, sneakers, a skateboard. Pick the thing and the artist you like.

  2. 02

    Share your inspiration

    Add pictures, links and notes in the inspiration tab, or point to a piece from the artist's showcase.

  3. 03

    Get a quote

    The artist replies with a price and delivery time. Nothing starts until you accept it.

  4. 04

    Receive and review

    Hand over or receive your item, approve the work, and leave a review.
